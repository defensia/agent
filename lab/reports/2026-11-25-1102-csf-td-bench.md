## Known lab-VM quirk found during this benchmark (not an agent/code bug)

`alma8-csf-cwp-orphan` (the TESTING=1, lfd-never-daemonises orphan VM)
reproducibly (3/3 runs) ends the 1000-entry prefill pass with CSF's own
cached `/etc/csf/csf.conf.error` state ("you appear to be missing a
required iptables module") after one `csf -td` call during the rapid
prefill loop fails to install its DROP rule. `iptables -L DENYIN -n` on
this VM showed an empty chain and `lsmod | grep xt_` showed almost no
netfilter extension modules loaded, on OrbStack's own custom kernel
(`7.2.9-orbstack-00391-...`) — this points at an OrbStack/kernel-module
characteristic of this specific golden clone, not something `csf -td`/
`-tr` (the agent's only two write operations) can trigger under normal,
much lower production ban rates. Recovered each time with a plain `csf -r`
(confirmed clean `csf -v` and `lab/verify.sh` green afterward). Not
something this plan fixes — flagged here and in `deferred-items.md` for
whoever next touches this VM's image.
